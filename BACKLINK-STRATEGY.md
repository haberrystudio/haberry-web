# Haberry Studio backlink strategy

Earn links and referral enquiries from businesses and organisations that already serve Kasaragod and Kerala. Lead with web development and digital marketing, where the agency can publish practical examples and answer common buyer questions.

| When | People to approach | What to offer | Page to link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Existing clients and collaborators, if any | A joint project write-up with approved screenshots, each team's contribution and verified results | The published case study, or the matching service page for a straightforward project credit |
| Days 1–30 | Kasaragod business associations and relevant local directories | An accurate membership or agency profile with a consistent business name, location and contact information | Homepage |
| Days 31–60 | Local business groups and event organisers, including relevant Kerala Startup Mission programmes | A practical session on preparing a small-business website or getting found in local search. Offer an original checklist for the event recap | The published checklist or relevant service page |
| Days 31–60 | Photographers, print studios, branding partners and business consultants | A client handoff guide that explains where each partner helps and when to bring them in | The guide or relevant service page |
| Days 61–90 | Kerala business newsletters and local publications | An original English or Malayalam resource such as a mobile website checklist for local shops, backed by examples you can document | The published resource |

Build a shortlist of 15 relevant organisations and send up to five personalised pitches per week. Prioritise publishers whose readers would use the material. For event outreach, start with the [Kerala Startup Mission locations page](https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in/contact) and [event listings](https://tickets.startupmission.in/).

Sample pitch

> Hi [name], I saw your [specific event or resource] for local businesses. We’re a digital agency in Kasaragod and have put together a checklist for [specific audience] covering [two practical items]. Here’s the public version [URL]. If it fits your [event recap or resources page], feel free to include it. I can share a short Malayalam version too if that would help your readers.

Record the organisation, contact, resource, destination, pitch date, reply, live referring URL, referral visits and enquiries. Review monthly in Google Search Console and your analytics. Use relevant referring domains and qualified referral enquiries to decide which outreach to repeat. Follow [Google's link spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) and give publishers editorial control over the link and its wording.
