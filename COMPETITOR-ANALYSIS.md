# Competitor analysis (November 2026)

Looked at: Acodez (Calicut/Delhi), Webandcrafts / WAC (Kochi), Inter Smart (Kochi), SRV Infotech (Kerala),
and the public profile of Pendagon Technologies (Kasaragod, founded 2024). I read each site's first screen and
home-page copy in a browser; I did not audit their speed, SEO rankings or inner pages. Their claims are theirs,
unverified by me.

## What they do

| Site | Hero says | Proof they lean on | Start-a-project path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acodez | "Top Notch. Award Winning. Affordable." | 16 awards, Google Partner, big-name portfolio | "Quick Enquiry" slide-out, floating WhatsApp |
| WAC | "Experiences Powered by Intelligence." (over a video) | 700+ projects, 1000+ clients, retail giants | Phone icon, contact page |
| Inter Smart | "Web Designing Company in Kochi, Kerala" (over an office photo) | 130+ experts, 1500+ clients, 2150+ projects | "Contact" page |
| SRV Infotech | "We are SRV and we don't wear capes" (same paragraph repeated on five slides) | "Best software company in Kerala" | Floating call and WhatsApp buttons |
| Pendagon (Kasaragod) | Directory copy: "360° digital solutions" | "We speak in numbers, not jargon" | Email |

## Their weaknesses (where HABERRY can win)

1. **The first screen does not say what they do or for whom.** Slogans ("Experiences Powered by Intelligence",
   "superheroes") or a bare keyword headline. A small-shop owner cannot tell if it is for them.
2. **Enterprise voice, enterprise proof.** Awards, global clients, thousands of projects. A Kasaragod trader
   cannot judge or relate to any of it. Several stats cannot be checked.
3. **Copy is generic and repeated.** Same sentence on every slide; "best", "top", "leading" with no evidence.
4. **No Malayalam on any of them.** Their audience asked for it.
5. **No price guidance and no budget conversation.** Nobody helps a first-timer figure out what they need.
6. **Starting is slow.** "Contact us" pages with long forms; WhatsApp is a floating button, not part of the flow.
7. **Heavy first loads:** video heroes, carousels, cookie/recaptcha overlays, stock office photos.
8. **No honest roadmap or boundaries.** Nobody says what they will not do.

## HABERRY's answer (what the site now does)

| Gap | HABERRY |
|---|---|
| Unclear first screen | H1 states what, for whom, where. Six tappable service tiles. Three ways to start in the first screen |
| Enterprise proof | No fake numbers. An explicit promise: "if we show it, it is real". Honest roadmap (Now vs Planned) |
| Generic copy | Plain-language "you get" lists; four working principles; FAQ answering real first questions |
| No Malayalam | Full Malayalam version with a clear English / മലയാളം switch (draft, needs native review) |
| No budget conversation | 30-second brief builder: what, goal, timing, budget band (optional), then WhatsApp or form |
| Slow start | Tap a tile → WhatsApp opens with your choice written; or finish the brief in about 30 seconds |
| Heavy pages | No video, no carousel, no stock photos, about 100 kB gzipped JS, lite mode for slow connections |
| No boundaries | FAQ says plainly what is available now, what is planned, and that past work will only be shown when real |

## What HABERRY still lacks (honest)

- **Real portfolio.** Every competitor has one. We show none rather than fake any. Add real case studies as soon
  as one client allows it; this is the biggest remaining gap.
- **Reviews / testimonials.** None yet. Add real ones with permission.
- **Team and founder faces.** Not supplied.
- **Domain, Google Business Profile, local SEO pages.** Competitors target "web design Kasaragod / Kochi"
  keywords; do the same once the domain is live.
